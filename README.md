# SIGNAL: A 2-Minute Cyberpsychology Game

A short interactive game about how people behave online, built for the course *Advanced Theories in Cyberpsychology*.

**Play it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/activity.html

## What is it?
Players move through seven quick levels and make fast choices: how to show up online, what to reach for, how to react to a scam, to a friend who was embarrassed, to screen-time rules, to new security rules, and to an online troll. Players are not told what is being measured. At the end, the game reveals a "signal type" and explains the psychology behind it.

## The psychology behind it
- **Carl Rogers:** people grow when they can be themselves and are accepted without judgment (being real and warmth).
- **Abraham Maslow:** needs come in layers: safety, belonging, recognition and growth.
- **Self-Determination Theory:** people thrive when they feel free to choose (autonomy), capable (competence) and close to others (relatedness).

## Why it matters for cybersecurity
People who feel accepted report their mistakes. People who feel in control follow the rules. People who feel capable spot scams. Good security protects the human, not just the computer.

## Privacy
Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

## Important note
This is an educational game and a conversation starter, not a psychological test or diagnosis.

## Customize it
Levels, text and timers can be edited in `activity.html`. The timers are set by `CHOICE_TIME` and `RUSH_TIME` near the bottom of the file.
