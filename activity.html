PLOT TWIST: THE GAME WAS A TEST

Every level secretly measured how you act online, using three big ideas from psychology:

  • Carl Rogers: People grow when they can be their real selves and are accepted without judgment. Levels 1, 4 and 7.
  • Abraham Maslow: We all have needs in layers: safety, belonging, recognition and growth. Level 2, the orb round.
  • Self-Determination Theory: Humans need three things to thrive: choice (autonomy), feeling capable (competence) and feeling close to others (relatedness). Levels 3, 5 and 6.
Why it matters for cybersecurity

People who feel accepted report their mistakes. People who feel in control follow the rules. People who feel capable spot scams. Good security protects the human, not just the computer.
